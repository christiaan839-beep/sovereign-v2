# Outreach delivery + follow-up cadence

The Loom does the selling. The email is just the wrapper that gets the
Loom watched. This file is the exact sequence — copy these templates,
swap the bracketed slots, send.

---

## Channel priority

For each founder on your ICP list, send in this order, stopping the
moment they reply:

1. **LinkedIn DM** (highest reply rate if they're active on LI)
2. **Email** with the Loom link inline (works for everyone)
3. **X DM** (only if they're active on X — check last 7 days)
4. **Followup email** at day 4
5. **Final breakup email** at day 9

If they haven't replied by day 12, archive them. Move on. Re-add to
the list in 90 days with a new hook.

---

## Email #1 — the Loom delivery (day 0)

### Subject line — pick one of these three (test which works for you)

- `quick question about [their company]`
- `[first name] — saw your post about [topic]`
- `90 sec for you — re: [their pain point]`

**Do not use:**

- ❌ "Quick chat?" — generic
- ❌ "Partnership opportunity" — instant delete
- ❌ "Re:" or "Fwd:" without a real prior thread — feels deceptive

### Body

```
Hey [first name],

Recorded a 90-second Loom for you specifically — about
[their specific pain / topic from your hook].

  ▶ [Loom URL]

The short version: I built an AI agent platform called Sovereign
Matrix, and I'm running it as a managed service for 5 founding
B2B SaaS customers this month — 50 qualified leads/month delivered
to your inbox, $4,999 setup + $999/month, money-back if I miss.

If the Loom resonates, here's the 15-min fit-call link:
[CALENDLY URL]

If not, no worries — appreciate the 90 seconds.

— [your first name]
[your title], Sovereign Matrix
[your domain].com/lead-engine
```

### Why this works

- **Loom link in the second line.** Don't bury it.
- **Three lines of context.** Founders skim — give them the answer to
  "what is this?" inside the email so they decide whether to watch.
- **Two clear CTAs.** Watch the Loom OR book the call. Both are
  instant decisions.
- **No "looking forward to hearing from you."** That's a tell of a
  spammer. Just sign off.
- **Public landing page in the signature.** Lets them check you out
  before responding.

## LinkedIn DM #1 — same day, before the email

LinkedIn caps DMs to about 1,900 chars in the connect note (300 if not
connected). Use this short version:

```
Hey [first name] — recorded a personalised 90-second Loom for you
about [their specific thing]:

[Loom URL]

Short version: I'm running my AI agent platform as a managed lead-gen
service. 50 qualified leads/month, $4,999 + $999, money-back. Taking
5 founding customers.

15-min fit call if it lands: [Calendly URL]

— [first name]
```

**Order of operations:** send the LinkedIn DM first (instant
notification), then the email 30 minutes later (catches them on a
different surface). Most founders reply on whichever pings them first.

---

## Email #2 — the followup (day 4)

If no reply, no Loom watch, send this 4 days after #1.

### Subject

Just reply to your own thread. Don't change the subject. The original
subject had their name + their topic, that's already optimised.

### Body

```
Hey [first name],

Bumping this in case it slid past — 90-sec Loom on getting you 50
qualified leads/month without an SDR:

  ▶ [Loom URL]

If you're not in the market for this right now, totally fine — just
reply "no" and I'll stop bumping.

— [your first name]
```

### Why this works

- **"Reply 'no' and I'll stop"** — gives them an easy out, which
  triggers reciprocity. About 5% will say "actually, tell me more."
- **No new pitch.** They already saw the offer. Don't re-explain.
- **Acknowledges they're busy.** Founders respect founders who don't
  guilt-trip.

## Email #3 — followup if Loom watched but no reply (day 4)

If Loom analytics show they watched 30%+ but didn't reply, **this is
your warm lead.** Different message:

### Subject

`saw you watched the Loom — quick thought`

### Body

```
[first name] — Loom showed you watched a chunk of the video. I know
that means it landed somehow but you're either busy or unsure.

Two quick options:

1. The 15-min fit call — no pitch, I'll show you 5 anonymised real
   leads from a current customer so you can see what the delivery
   actually looks like:
   [Calendly URL]

2. Or reply with one question and I'll answer in writing — happy to
   give you anything you'd need to decide.

Either way, no pressure.

— [first name]
```

This converts at roughly 25%. **Watch your Loom analytics daily and
trigger this email within 2 hours of detecting a watch.**

---

## Email #4 — the breakup (day 9)

Final email. Counter-intuitively the highest-reply-rate one in many
sequences, because it removes urgency.

### Subject

`closing the loop, [first name]`

### Body

```
Hey [first name] —

Going to assume the timing isn't right and stop emailing. Genuinely no
hard feelings — I know cold pitches are noise.

If anything changes, the door's open: [Calendly URL]

Best of luck with [specific thing about their company — recent
launch, hiring, pivot — proves you're paying attention].

— [first name]
```

### Why this works

- **It's a real breakup, not a fake one.** No "circling back next
  quarter" — that's spam. Mean what you say.
- **References something specific they're working on.** Last chance
  to prove you're a human.
- **Door open without pressure.** Some respond a week later because
  the urgency is gone.

Expected reply rate on the breakup: 8–15%. Of those, half are "thanks
for understanding," but the other half are "actually, can we talk
next week?"

## X / Twitter DM (parallel channel — day 0 or day 2)

Only send if they tweeted in the last 7 days. If their last tweet was
3 weeks ago, skip — they don't check.

```
Hey [first name] — saw your tweet about [topic]. Recorded a 90-sec
Loom for you specifically:
[Loom URL]
50 qualified B2B SaaS leads/month, managed service. 15-min fit call
if it lands: [Calendly URL]
```

X DMs work best when shorter than your email. People skim them
between scrolling. Keep it 3 lines max.

## What to do when they reply

### Reply: "Interesting, tell me more"

Send the Calendly link, ONE line:

```
Easiest way to walk through it is the 15-min fit call. Three slots
this week:
[Calendly URL]
```

Resist the urge to write a 3-paragraph response. The call is the
point — protect it.

### Reply: "What's the price again?"

```
$4,999 one-time setup + $999/month. Month-to-month, cancel any time.
30-day money-back on the first batch — if I deliver fewer than 50
qualified leads I refund the setup, no questions.

If the price works, I'd hop on a call to make sure the rest is a fit:
[Calendly URL]
```

### Reply: "Can you send a sample first?"

DO NOT send a 50-lead sample. That's a $4,999 deliverable.

```
Yep — on the fit call I'll walk through 5 real anonymised leads from
a current customer with full enrichment, qualification logic, and
outreach copy. That's the closest preview I can show without giving
away the customer's actual prospects.

[Calendly URL]
```

### Reply: "We already use [Apollo/Clay/Smartlead]"

```
Most of my customers do too. The difference is they're paying me to
*run* those tools instead of paying someone in-house to do it. The
output is the same shape (leads + outreach copy in your inbox); the
difference is who does the work.

Happy to compare side-by-side on the fit call if useful:
[Calendly URL]
```

### Reply: "Send me a proposal"

DO NOT send a written proposal. That's a closing-killer.

```
Proposals usually run me a couple hours and I'd rather make sure
it's a fit before either of us invests that. The 15 min on a call
is the proposal — I'll walk through scope, deliverables, timeline,
and pricing live, and you'll have everything you need to decide.

[Calendly URL]
```

### Reply: "Not right now, maybe in [N months]"

```
Totally — happy to circle back in [their timeframe]. One quick
favour: if you know a founder who *is* feeling the lead-gen pain
right now, would be hugely helpful if you could intro me. Most of my
customers come from referrals.

— [first name]
```

About 1 in 6 of these "not now" replies generate a referral. That's
your real ROI on this channel.

## Daily admin (15 min/day, evening)

End of every day:

1. Open the spreadsheet.
2. Update the "Watched", "Watched %", "Replied" columns from Loom
   analytics + your email.
3. Trigger any Email #3s for Loom-watchers who didn't reply.
4. Schedule any Email #2s and #4s for tomorrow morning.
5. Add 10 fresh names to the list for tomorrow's Loom batch.

That's the whole thing. **The system is boring on purpose.** It's
the boring consistency that hits $30k.
