# Building the ICP-150 list

The 150 founders you'll Loom over the next 30 days. Get this list right
and the outreach almost runs itself. Get it wrong and 90% of your Looms
land on the wrong desk.

---

## The exact ICP for Sovereign Lead Engine

You're looking for founders who match **all six** of these. If a name
fails any one, drop them.

| Filter          | Range                                                                                                | Why                                                           |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Vertical**    | B2B SaaS — sells software to other businesses                                                        | They have ACV > $1k/year, which makes the unit economics work |
| **Headcount**   | 1–50 employees                                                                                       | Small enough to need leads, big enough to afford $999/mo      |
| **ARR**         | $0 – $2M                                                                                             | Below $0 they can't pay; above $2M they have a sales team     |
| **Role**        | Founder / CEO / Co-founder                                                                           | They sign cheques. SDRs and growth managers don't.            |
| **Pain signal** | Posted about lead-gen, hired SDR recently, complained about agencies, doing cold outreach themselves | Pain = budget                                                 |
| **Geography**   | English-speaking (US, UK, AU, CA, NZ, EU)                                                            | Outreach copy is English-first                                |

If you serve South Africa specifically, replicate this list with local
filters and use Yoco for billing instead of PayPal.

## Where to source 150 founders fast (in priority order)

The first 30 should come from your warmest pools. The next 120 are
cold but precision-targeted.

### Tier 1 — your warm network (target: 30 names, ~30 min)

These convert at 3-5× cold. Mine them first.

1. **Your LinkedIn 1st-degree connections** — filter by job title
   "Founder" + company size 2-50. Export to CSV.
2. **People who liked / commented on your last 5 posts.** They already
   know you exist. Massive head start.
3. **Indie Hackers profile views** — IH shows you who's been on your
   profile. Many are founders kicking tires.
4. **Anyone you've replied to on X in the last 6 months.** Re-engage.
5. **Your existing Sovereign Matrix waitlist / signup list.** If you
   have one, this is gold.

### Tier 2 — adjacent communities (target: 60 names, ~3 hours)

These are cold but high-intent.

6. **Indie Hackers founders shipping in B2B SaaS.** Filter the IH
   directory by "B2B" tag, $0-$2M ARR.
7. **Recent YC W24/S24/W25/S25 batches** at the seed/pre-A stage.
   YC founders are public, easy to find on LinkedIn.
8. **Founders posting about cold outreach pain** on X / LinkedIn —
   search the literal phrases:
   - "looking for an SDR"
   - "our outbound isn't working"
   - "fired our agency"
   - "Apollo not converting"
   - "how do you do lead gen"
9. **Recent Product Hunt B2B SaaS launches** with under 200 upvotes —
   founders are still hands-on and need leads.
10. **MicroAcquire / Acquire.com listings** — micro-SaaS owners who
    just bought a company need to grow it.

### Tier 3 — outbound databases (target: 60 names, ~4 hours)

Pure cold but volume targeting.

11. **Apollo.io free tier** — filter: B2B SaaS, 2-50 employees,
    Founder/CEO, US/UK/CA/AU. Export 60 names.
12. **Crunchbase** — recent Seed / Series A B2B SaaS rounds in last
    90 days. Founders need to grow into the round.
13. **PitchBook free** equivalent if you have access.

If you don't want to pay for Apollo, **skip Tier 3 entirely** and
spend more time deepening Tier 1+2. Quality > quantity.

## The spreadsheet schema (use this exact layout)

Open Google Sheets. Create one tab per tier. Columns:

| #   | Column             | Required     | Notes                                               |
| --- | ------------------ | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | First Name         | yes          | First name only — never "Mr."                       |
| 2   | Last Name          | yes          |                                                     |
| 3   | Company            | yes          |                                                     |
| 4   | Role               | yes          | Filter: must contain "founder", "CEO", "co-founder" |
| 5   | LinkedIn URL       | yes          | For Loom thumbnail + DM fallback                    |
| 6   | Email              | yes          | Use Apollo / Hunter / RocketReach                   |
| 7   | X / Twitter handle | optional     | Useful for the personal hook                        |
| 8   | Company URL        | yes          | For the screen-share hook                           |
| 9   | ARR estimate       | optional     | $0 / $0-100k / $100k-1M / $1M+                      |
| 10  | Headcount          | yes          | LinkedIn employee count                             |
| 11  | **Personal hook**  | **yes**      | The single most important field. See below.         |
| 12  | Source             | yes          | Which tier did this come from                       |
| 13  | Date sourced       | yes          |                                                     |
| 14  | Loom URL           | filled later |                                                     |
| 15  | Sent at            | filled later |                                                     |
| 16  | Watched?           | filled later | yes / no                                            |
| 17  | Watched %          | filled later | from Loom analytics                                 |
| 18  | Replied?           | filled later | yes / no                                            |
| 19  | Booked call?       | filled later | yes / no                                            |
| 20  | Closed $?          | filled later | $4,999 + $999 mark                                  |

The "Personal hook" column is non-negotiable. **If you can't fill it in
under 90 seconds of research, drop the name and move on.** The hook
should be specific enough that another founder reading it would
recognise the person.

## How to find the "Personal hook" in under 90 seconds

For each name, in order, until you have one:

1. **LinkedIn last 30 days posts** — what did they post about?
2. **X last 30 days** — any rant / question / win they've shared?
3. **Their company blog** — recent post they wrote?
4. **Their podcast appearances** — anything in last 90 days?
5. **Their hiring page** — what role are they trying to fill?
6. **Their pricing page** — what jumps out (high prices, weird tiers)?

Examples of strong hooks:

- "Saw your post on Tuesday about ditching Apollo — that's why I'm
  reaching out."
- "Your changelog mentions you just shipped self-serve onboarding —
  congrats on that."
- "Listened to your episode on the SaaS Podcast about churn — your
  framework was sharp."

Examples of WEAK hooks (do not use):

- ❌ "Saw you're a founder of a SaaS company" — that's not a hook
- ❌ "I noticed your company on LinkedIn" — useless
- ❌ "Hope you're doing well!" — instant delete

## Daily targeting rhythm (so you ship 10 Looms/day)

| Day-of-week   | Focus                                   | Output                               |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Monday**    | Tier 1 only                             | 10 Looms to warmest names            |
| **Tuesday**   | Tier 1 + Tier 2                         | 10 Looms                             |
| **Wednesday** | Tier 2 (IH + YC)                        | 10 Looms                             |
| **Thursday**  | Tier 2 (cold-pain searchers)            | 10 Looms                             |
| **Friday**    | Tier 1 followups + new Tier 2           | 10 Looms + replies                   |
| **Saturday**  | Off, but reply to anything that came in | replies only                         |
| **Sunday**    | List-building for next week             | refill spreadsheet to 30 fresh names |

Consistency beats sprints. **10 Looms × 5 days × 3 weeks = 150 Looms.**
You will book 8–15 fit calls. You will close 3–5 customers. That's
your $30k month.

## Red flags — do NOT add these to the list

Even if they look good on paper.

- 🚩 **Over 50 employees** — they have a sales team, you'll lose to
  the existing process.
- 🚩 **Recently raised Series A/B** — they're hiring an in-house
  team, not buying a managed service.
- 🚩 **Service-based business** (agency, consultancy) — they sell
  retainers, not subs; your unit economics don't fit theirs.
- 🚩 **B2C founders** — paid ads outperform what you can do.
- 🚩 **Devtools sold to engineers under $20/mo** — ACV too low for
  the $999/mo math to work.
- 🚩 **Founders publicly anti-AI** — easy to spot from their posts.
  Save the bandwidth.

The list isn't the goal. **150 Looms sent + 5 closes** is the goal.
