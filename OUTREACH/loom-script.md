# 90-second Loom script — Sovereign Lead Engine

The single piece of content that does most of the work in the $30k month.
Record this once for your own reference, then re-record a personalised
version for each of the 150 founders on your ICP list.

---

## Format rules (don't skip these — they make or break the conversion)

- **Length: 90 seconds max.** 60 is better. Anything past 90 gets skipped.
- **Face-on-camera.** Ring light or window. Founders can spot a stock-video
  pitch in 2 seconds.
- **Background visible.** Books, plants, your kitchen — anything human.
  Sterile blank-wall = "this is automated."
- **First three seconds say their name and one specific thing about them.**
  "Hey Sarah, I just read your post about churn at Loom-y." That single
  sentence is the difference between 3% and 30% reply rates.
- **One call to action only.** Don't say "book a call OR reply OR DM me
  back." Just the Calendly link.
- **End with a 3-second pause** so they can't skip the CTA before the
  Loom autoplays the next thing.
- **Send via Loom.com link, not file attachment.** Loom shows you who
  watched, for how long, and on what device. That data drives followups.

## The script (60–90 seconds)

> **0:00 — Personal hook (10 sec)**
>
> Hey [first name], it's [your first name]. I saw your post on
> [LinkedIn / X / IH / podcast] about [specific thing — their churn
> > issue, their funding round, their hiring spree, their tweet about
> > SDRs]. That's actually why I'm reaching out.

> **0:10 — The problem you solve (15 sec)**
>
> You're [stage of company], probably trying to figure out lead-gen
> without dropping $120k on an SDR or $5k/mo on an agency that ghosts
> you after week three. I built something for that exact problem.

> **0:25 — What it is (15 sec)**
>
> It's called Sovereign Lead Engine. Every Monday you get 50 qualified
> B2B SaaS prospects in your inbox — verified emails, recent buying
> signals, and personalised outreach copy ready to send. You don't
> learn a tool. You don't manage a contractor. You just send and book
> meetings.

> **0:40 — The proof (15 sec)**
>
> I'm running it on [your own company OR an early customer]. We're
> averaging [X meetings booked per 50 leads delivered]. Not magic —
> just an AI agent stack I built over 12 months that handles the
> sourcing, enrichment, and copy at the same level a senior SDR would.

> **0:55 — The ask (15 sec)**
>
> I'm taking 5 founding customers this month at $4,999 setup plus
> $999/month, with a 30-day money-back guarantee on the first batch.
> If you'd want to be one of them, the link below the video is a
> 15-minute fit call. If we're not a fit, I'll tell you in the call,
> and you keep your time.

> **1:10 — Sign-off (5 sec)**
>
> Either way — appreciate you giving me 90 seconds. Talk soon.

> **(2-second silent pause, then end recording)**

## Personalisation cheat sheet

For every Loom you record, change these four things. Everything else
stays identical:

| Slot                 | What to change                                                                             | Where to find it                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| First name           | Theirs                                                                                     | LinkedIn / Twitter / their site            |
| The "specific thing" | Something they posted in the last 14 days                                                  | Their X feed, LinkedIn, recent podcast     |
| Company size cue     | "8-person company", "post-seed startup", "5 engineers"                                     | LinkedIn employee count                    |
| Pain reference       | Match it to their stage — early = "no SDR budget", later = "your last SDR didn't work out" | Their hiring page, glassdoor, recent posts |

If you can't find a personal hook in 90 seconds of research, **skip
that founder.** You're aiming for 150 high-effort Looms, not 500
generic ones.

## Recording workflow (so 10 Looms / day is sustainable)

1. **Batch the research.** Spend 2 hours one morning building your
   ICP-150 spreadsheet with the personal hook column pre-filled.
   Do not record while researching — you'll hate it within 3 days.
2. **Record in 30-min sprints.** Open the spreadsheet. Hit record.
   Read the script. Stop. Click "copy share link." Paste into the
   email/LinkedIn DM template (see `email-sequences.md`). Send.
   Move to next row.
3. **No retakes after the first one.** The first Loom of the day will
   be your worst. Send it anyway. You're not auditioning, you're
   talking to a human.
4. **Track in the spreadsheet.** Columns: name, company, Loom URL,
   sent at, watched at, watched %, replied, booked.
5. **Reply to every reply within 1 hour during business hours.** This
   single behaviour doubles your booking rate.

## Two upgrades that double conversion

Once you've sent your first 30 Looms, layer these in:

1. **Open with a 1-second screen-share of their site** before flipping
   to your face. "Hey Sarah, just looking at lemonade.com/pricing right
   now…" — instantly proves you're not blasting a list.
2. **Custom thumbnail.** Loom lets you set a custom Loom thumbnail
   (their logo + your face). The click-through rate goes from 28% to
   ~50% with a personalised thumbnail.

## What NOT to say

- ❌ "Just wanted to reach out" — every cold pitch starts this way.
  Skip it.
- ❌ "Quick question" — they know it's not a quick question.
- ❌ "I help B2B SaaS founders unlock their lead pipeline" — generic
  positioning copy. Founders tune out instantly.
- ❌ "Got 5 minutes for a chat?" — vague ask, vague no. Always offer
  a specific outcome (50 leads in 30 days) and a specific commitment
  (15-min call).
- ❌ Mentioning that it's an AI platform first. Lead with the outcome.
  The fact that it's AI underneath is implementation detail.

## Expected numbers (so you know if it's working)

After your first 30 Looms, you should see roughly:

| Metric               | Target | Below this means                                    |
| -------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Loom watch rate      | 30%+   | Personalisation isn't strong enough                 |
| Watch-to-reply rate  | 15%+   | The pitch is unclear or the ask is too big          |
| Reply-to-call-booked | 50%+   | The Calendly link is broken / friction in booking   |
| Calls-to-close       | 30%+   | Pricing is wrong or you're not asking for the close |

If your first 10 Looms get **zero** watches, the issue is the subject
line / opener of your delivery message, not the Loom itself. Iterate
the hook before recording 90 more.

If you get 30%+ watch rates but 0% replies, the issue is the offer or
the CTA — re-record with a stronger guarantee or a smaller next step.
