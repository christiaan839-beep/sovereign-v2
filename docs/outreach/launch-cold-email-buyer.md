# Launch cold email — compliance-conscious buyer

For SA legal / financial / healthcare CTOs and Heads of Compliance.
Adjacent ICPs: EU mid-market regulated industries (insurance,
pharma, banking) where POPIA / EU AI Act audit-trail requirements
block AI deployment.

Distinct from the existing `cold-emails.md` (which is hiring-
oriented). This one's about **selling the platform**, not getting hired.

## Personalization slot — what to put in [SPECIFIC SIGNAL]

The single sentence that proves you're not blasting. Sources:

- LinkedIn post / share in last 60 days about AI, compliance,
  POPIA, GDPR, or EU AI Act
- Conference talk listed on their profile (Compliance Africa,
  AI Risk Summit, RegTech Connect, ISACA SA chapter events)
- Open job req on their careers page that mentions AI risk,
  AI governance, AI compliance, or "responsible AI"
- Recent press release or LinkedIn announcement mentioning AI
  adoption / "AI strategy" / "ML in production"
- Their company's most recent annual report / sustainability
  report — search for "artificial intelligence" or "automated
  decision"

Bad: "I see you work in compliance."
Good: "Saw your Compliance Africa 2026 talk on AI traceability —
the part where you said 'we just can't ship without a paper
trail' is exactly what I built this for."

## Send pattern

Day 1: Send email. Send LinkedIn connection request with a 200-
char DM that mirrors the email's first 1–2 sentences. Different
channel, same message, doubles surface.

Day 4: If no reply, **forward your own email** with one line:
"Bumping this — open to a quick 15? I can keep it under 15."

Day 8: If still no reply, send one final follow-up linking to a
new asset. Something like a blog post you've published since,
or a customer case study. Then stop.

Day 21: Move them to "no signal" bucket. Don't email again for
6 months minimum.

## Tracking template

Add a row per send to a spreadsheet:

| Sent       | Name       | Role               | Company    | Industry | Loom views    | Reply? | Notes             |
| ---------- | ---------- | ------------------ | ---------- | -------- | ------------- | ------ | ----------------- |
| 2026-05-11 | Jane Smith | Head of Compliance | ACME Legal | Legal    | (set by Loom) | (Y/N)  | "in EU expansion" |

After 30 sends, calculate:

- Reply rate. <2% = subject lines or personalization is failing.
- Loom view rate. <40% of opens viewing the Loom = thumbnail or
  subject is weak.
- Demo conversion. <30% of replies converting to demos = ask
  is unclear or trust signal is weak.
