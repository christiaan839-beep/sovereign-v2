# AI workflow disclosure (reusable footer)

Per Anthropic's [candidate AI guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/candidate-ai-guidance),
disclosure of AI tool use is expected and welcome. The block below goes
at the bottom of every cold email and on the `/now` page. Do not remove
it — disclosure is a positive signal, not a liability.

## Long form (cold email footer, ~50 words)

```
─────
AI workflow disclosure (per Anthropic's candidate AI guidance)
I pair-program with Claude Code on Sovereign Matrix. Architecture,
security, and merge decisions are mine; Claude generates code under my
direction. Every PR carries a Claude attribution trailer. Receipts:
sovereignmatrix.agency/now → "Recently shipped" links to file paths.
```

## Short form (LinkedIn DM footer, ~20 words)

```
AI-pair-programming disclosed per Anthropic candidate guidance — receipts at sovereignmatrix.agency/now.
```

## One-line form (resume / portfolio bottom)

```
AI workflow: Claude Code pair programming, fully disclosed.
```

## Where disclosure goes

- Every cold email — long form
- Every LinkedIn DM — short form
- Every PR description — Claude attribution trailer
- `/now` page — explicit "Not open to" item ("Anything that asks me to abandon the AI workflow disclosure")
- Resume bottom — one-line form
- Interview opening — verbal mention before deep dives

## Why this is a feature, not a flaw

1. **It signals judgment about tooling.** The candidates who hide AI
   use are the ones being filtered out. The ones who disclose
   thoughtfully are the ones being short-listed.
2. **It matches the team you want.** Anthropic, Cognition, Vercel —
   their own engineers ship with AI assistance. They want hires who
   can articulate the workflow.
3. **It pre-empts the question.** If you don't bring it up, they will,
   and the conversation gets defensive. Bring it up first and frame it.

## Don't say

- "I used AI to build this" (hides the workflow)
- "AI did the work" (untrue and signals lack of ownership)
- "Built entirely by hand" (untrue if you used Claude)

## Do say

- "I pair-program with Claude. Architecture and merges are mine."
- "Every PR has a Claude attribution trailer."
- "Receipts at /now."
