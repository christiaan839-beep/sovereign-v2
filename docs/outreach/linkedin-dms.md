## DM-specific rules

- **No subject** — LinkedIn doesn't show one in the inbox preview.
  First sentence IS the hook.
- **No long signature** — name is already on profile.
- **No long disclosure block** — the short form ("AI-pair-programming
  disclosed per Anthropic candidate guidance") is enough; full
  disclosure goes in the email sent the same day.
- **Profile picture matters** — DM goes nowhere if your profile is
  empty. Make sure profile shows: title ("Engineer building Sovereign
  Matrix"), location ("Cape Town"), one-line bio, link to /now.
- **Same-day pairing** — send email first (lands in inbox), DM second
  (lands in LinkedIn). The combination doubles surface area without
  feeling like spam, because it's two distinct channels.

## What NOT to do on LinkedIn

- Connection request → "Thanks for connecting" → pitch a week later.
  This is the dead pattern. Skip the connection step; InMail directly.
- Voice notes — almost nobody listens; risks looking unserious.
- "Quick question" subject lines (when you're using an InMail with a
  subject) — recipients have learned this is always a sales pitch.
- Asking for a referral. Ask for 20 minutes; let them offer the
  referral.

## Send checklist (every DM)

- [ ] Under 300 characters total
- [ ] First sentence is a specific observation about their work
- [ ] One link, one ask, one disclosure phrase
- [ ] Sent the same day as the email (not a week apart)
- [ ] Profile is complete and consistent with the email's claims
