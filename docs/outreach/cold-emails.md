# Cold emails — five templates

Each template is < 120 words, has one specific signal slot, one link to
evidence, one ask, one disclosure footer. Subject lines under 60 chars.

**Slot legend:**

- `{{first_name}}` — recipient's first name
- `{{role_title}}` — exact role title from their careers page
- `{{role_signal}}` — one specific public observation about THEIR work
- `{{company}}` — recipient's company
- `{{cal_link}}` — your calendar link (e.g. cal.com/sovereign-matrix)

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Generic openings: "I hope this email finds you well", "I came across
  your profile", "I was browsing your careers page"
- Adjective stacks: "world-class," "elite," "premier," "passionate,"
  "results-driven"
- Multiple CTAs: pick one — calendar OR reply OR portfolio link
- Listing every tech stack item — pick the three that matter for the
  recipient and cut the rest
- Long sign-offs: "Best regards / Warmest wishes / Looking forward to
  hearing from you" — `— Christiaan` is enough
- Apologizing for cold emailing — apology pre-frames you as imposing,
  which lowers reply rate

## Send checklist (every email)

- [ ] Subject < 60 chars
- [ ] Body < 120 words
- [ ] One specific signal about THEIR work in the first sentence
- [ ] One link to evidence (not three)
- [ ] One ask (20 min + calendar link)
- [ ] Disclosure footer included
- [ ] Recipient name spelled correctly
- [ ] Sent from the address that matches your portfolio domain
