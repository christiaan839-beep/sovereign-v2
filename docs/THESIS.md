# THESIS

The single paragraph that decides every other decision. If a roadmap
item, a feature, a hire, or a customer doesn't pass this paragraph,
it doesn't ship.

> \*\*Sovereign Matrix is for B2B SaaS founders running a 1–50-person
> company who need a steady stream of qualified prospects but can't
> justify a $120k/year SDR or a $5k/month agency that ghosts after
> week three. They will pay $4,999 + $999/month for 50 hand-reviewed
> leads in their inbox every Monday, with a 30-day money-back
> guarantee. Over time, the same engine becomes the trust + memory
>
> - outcome layer for any team running autonomous AI workflows —
>   open-source verifier protocol, per-tenant memory moat, A2E
>   marketplace. The bet is a decade.\*\*

---

## What this thesis commits us to

**The customer.** A specific person at a specific stage. Not "AI
operators." Not "growth teams." A founder of a B2B SaaS company
between pre-revenue and ~$2M ARR. Someone who is doing their own
outbound poorly, or paid an agency that didn't work, and would
rather hire a person than another tool.

**The price.** $4,999 setup + $999/month. Not $99. Not $19. The
price filters tire-kickers, attracts buyers, sustains hand-touched
delivery, and signals that we're not a SaaS — we're a managed
service backed by a platform. If the price scares you, you're
not the customer.

**The cadence.** Weekly. Monday 9am their timezone, every week, no
exceptions. Miss it, the month is on us. STANDARDS.md §03 is the
single most-tested standard in the codebase.

**The guarantee.** 50 qualified leads in the first 30 days OR a
no-friction same-day refund of the setup fee. Most platforms hide
behind "the AI tried." We stand behind results.

**The wedge.** B2B SaaS lead-gen. Not 5 verticals. Not 3 products.
One vertical, one outcome, until $30k MRR. Then we earn the right
to expand.

**The decade.** This is not a 6-month experiment. The companies
that age into legendary status (Stripe, Linear, Shopify, Figma,
Postgres) all required a decade of compounding. We're committed to
that arc. Every quarterly decision is checked against "will this
matter in 2036?"

---

## What this thesis explicitly is NOT

- **Not "an AI agent platform for everyone."** That market is
  already commoditising. We're a managed service for one customer
  type, backed by a platform that becomes an open protocol over
  time.

- **Not a self-serve SaaS at $19/mo.** The platform tier exists
  for engineers who want raw access, but the revenue thesis is
  the $999/mo Lead Engine. We can shut down the $19/mo tier with
  zero impact on the business.

- **Not "AI for everything."** We do one thing — help B2B SaaS
  founders find their next customer — and we do it weekly,
  hand-reviewed, with a guarantee.

- **Not a marketplace from day 1.** The A2E protocol exists in
  the code; the marketplace launches in year 4 once we have 100+
  paying customers and 50+ third-party agent developers. Anyone
  who wants to ship the marketplace before then is wrong.

- **Not white-label as the primary model.** The agency tier
  ($499 platform pricing) earns enterprise revenue, but the wedge
  customer is a founder, not an agency.

---

## The acceptance criteria for any new direction

Before adding a vertical, building a feature, or saying yes to a
customer who doesn't fit, we re-read this paragraph. If the new
thing doesn't help us serve that exact founder more reliably, we
don't do it. If it explicitly contradicts the thesis (e.g., dropping
the guarantee, lowering the price below $999, taking on a B2C
customer), we don't do it.

The thesis is allowed to evolve once a year, in writing, with a
documented reason. The thesis is not allowed to drift without that
formal step. Drift is how startups become Wikipedia entries about
"companies that almost made it."

---

## The reading rhythm

- **Every Friday** before publishing the Friday Letter, re-read
  this file. If anything done this week contradicted the thesis,
  the Friday Letter names it.
- **Every quarter** explicitly answer: "Are we still on this
  thesis? What evidence supports / refutes it?" Update the
  document if needed.
- **Every annual review** ratify the thesis or update it with a
  written rationale. Keep the previous version in git so the
  evolution is visible.

The discipline of re-reading is the whole game. Most founders
write the thesis once and never look at it. The ones who do look
are the ones who become legendary.
