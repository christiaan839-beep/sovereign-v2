## MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement (**"MSA"**) is entered into as of
**[EFFECTIVE_DATE]** (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between:

- **Sovereign Matrix**, a [ENTITY_FORM] with its principal place of
  business at [ADDRESS] (**"Sovereign"**, **"we"**, or **"us"**); and
- **[CUSTOMER_NAME]**, a [CUSTOMER_ENTITY_FORM] with its principal
  place of business at [CUSTOMER_ADDRESS] (**"Customer"**, **"you"**).

Each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Services

1.1 Sovereign will provide the Customer access to the Sovereign Matrix
platform (the **"Service"**), described at `sovereignmatrix.agency/docs`
and supplemented by any Order Form signed between the Parties.

1.2 The Service is hosted, updated, and maintained by Sovereign. No
on-premises installation is provided under this MSA.

## 2. Term

2.1 This MSA starts on the Effective Date and continues until
terminated per Section 11.

2.2 Each Order Form specifies its own Subscription Term. Unless
either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least [30] days
before the Subscription Term ends, it will auto-renew for successive
periods of equal length.

## 3. Fees & payment

3.1 **Fees**. Customer will pay the fees specified on the Order Form.
Unless otherwise stated, fees are in US dollars, exclusive of taxes,
and payable via Stripe per the payment schedule on the Order Form.

3.2 **Taxes**. Customer is responsible for any sales, use, VAT, or
similar taxes, except taxes on Sovereign's net income.

3.3 **Late payment**. Undisputed invoices not paid within 30 days may
accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by
applicable law, whichever is lower. Sovereign may suspend the Service
after 60 days of non-payment upon 10 days' written notice.

3.4 **Price changes**. Sovereign may change fees at renewal upon 60
days' written notice. [Enterprise customers: price-lock clause if
negotiated.]

## 4. Customer data

4.1 **Ownership**. Customer retains all rights, title, and interest in
Customer Data. Sovereign obtains no ownership of Customer Data by
processing it through the Service.

4.2 **License to process**. Customer grants Sovereign a non-exclusive,
worldwide license to use, process, and display Customer Data solely to
provide the Service and as described in the Data Processing Agreement
attached as Exhibit A (**"DPA"**).

4.3 **Training data**. Sovereign does NOT use Customer Data to train
its own models or to improve general AI capabilities. Sovereign may
use aggregated, anonymized metrics for service operations.

4.4 **Deletion**. Upon termination or on Customer's written request,
Sovereign will delete Customer Data within 30 days, except where
retention is required by law or for our legitimate audit purposes
(billing records up to 7 years).

## 5. Customer obligations

Customer will:

5.1 Use the Service only for lawful purposes;

5.2 Comply with Sovereign's [Acceptable Use Policy](https://sovereignmatrix.agency/acceptable-use);

5.3 Keep credentials confidential and notify Sovereign promptly of
any suspected unauthorized access;

5.4 Not attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or extract proprietary
components of the Service;

5.5 Not use the Service to build a competing AI agent platform.

## 6. Intellectual property

6.1 Sovereign retains all rights in the Service, including software,
APIs, documentation, and branding.

6.2 Customer retains all rights in its data, templates, and outputs
generated through the Service.

6.3 Each Party retains the right to use general knowledge, skills, and
experience gained during the engagement.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party may access the other's **"Confidential Information"** —
non-public technical, business, or financial information identified as
confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as confidential.

7.2 Obligations:
- Use Confidential Information only to perform this MSA;
- Protect it with at least the same care used for own confidential
  information (but no less than reasonable care);
- Limit access to employees and contractors who need to know;
- Survive this MSA for [3] years after termination.

7.3 Exclusions: information that is publicly known, independently
developed, or received lawfully from a third party.

## 8. Security

8.1 Sovereign will maintain administrative, technical, and physical
safeguards designed to protect Customer Data. Current safeguards are
described in the Security Addendum at
`docs/security/SECURITY_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`.

8.2 Sovereign will notify Customer within 72 hours of becoming aware
of any **"Security Incident"** (a confirmed unauthorized access to
Customer Data).

8.3 Sovereign will maintain SOC 2 Type I attestation by end of Q3 2026,
and SOC 2 Type II by end of Q1 2027. [Enterprise customers: contractual
commitment with credits on failure.]

## 9. Service Level Agreement

9.1 **Availability target**: 99.5% monthly for standard plans, 99.9%
monthly for Enterprise plans measured via Sovereign's public status
page.

9.2 **Exclusions** from downtime: scheduled maintenance (notified 48
hours in advance), third-party provider outages, force majeure.

9.3 **Credits**: If uptime falls below the target, Customer receives a
service credit of [10%] for each full percentage point below target,
up to [50%] of the monthly fee. Credits are the sole remedy for
downtime.

## 10. Warranties & disclaimers

10.1 Each Party represents that it has authority to enter this MSA.

10.2 Sovereign warrants that the Service will perform substantially
as described in the documentation. Remedy: re-perform the failing
Service or, if not feasible, refund fees for the affected period.

10.3 **EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS"
WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR
PURPOSE, OR NON-INFRINGEMENT. SOVEREIGN DOES NOT WARRANT THAT AI
OUTPUTS WILL BE ACCURATE, COMPLETE, OR SUITABLE FOR ANY PARTICULAR
PURPOSE. CUSTOMER IS RESPONSIBLE FOR REVIEWING AI OUTPUTS BEFORE
RELYING ON THEM.**

## 11. Termination

11.1 **For convenience**: Either Party may terminate this MSA or any
Order Form on 30 days' written notice. Pre-paid fees are non-refundable
unless otherwise agreed.

11.2 **For cause**: Either Party may terminate immediately on written
notice if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within 30
days of written notice.

11.3 **Survival**: Sections 3 (payment of accrued fees), 4.1–4.4, 6,
7, 10.3, 12, and 13 survive termination.

## 12. Limitation of liability

12.1 **EXCLUSION**: Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental,
special, consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost
profits or revenues.

12.2 **CAP**: Each Party's total aggregate liability under this MSA
is limited to the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding
the event giving rise to the claim.

12.3 **EXCLUSIONS FROM CAP**: The cap does not apply to (a) breaches
of Section 7 (Confidentiality), (b) a Party's indemnification
obligations, or (c) Customer's payment obligations.

## 13. Indemnification

13.1 **By Sovereign**: Sovereign will defend and indemnify Customer
against third-party claims that the Service (as delivered) infringes
an intellectual property right. Remedies: modify the Service, procure
a license, or refund prepaid fees.

13.2 **By Customer**: Customer will defend and indemnify Sovereign
against third-party claims arising from Customer Data, Customer's
misuse of the Service, or violation of Section 5.

## 14. General

14.1 **Governing law**: [Delaware / California — negotiable per deal].

14.2 **Dispute resolution**: Parties will attempt good-faith negotiation
for 30 days before initiating any action. Any proceeding will be in
[STATE], [COUNTRY].

14.3 **Notices**: In writing, by email to legal@sovereignmatrix.agency
and the Customer email on the Order Form.

14.4 **Assignment**: Neither Party may assign this MSA without the
other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger, acquisition,
or sale of substantially all assets.

14.5 **Entire agreement**: This MSA, Order Forms, and the DPA form the
entire agreement. Any Customer purchase order terms that conflict are
expressly rejected.

14.6 **Amendments**: Only valid if in writing and signed by both
Parties.

14.7 **Severability**: If a provision is unenforceable, the rest
remains in effect.

## Common customer redlines we pre-accept

1. Data residency: we can do EU-only hosting for +30% infrastructure fee
2. Audit rights: 1 audit per year with 30-day notice, at customer expense
3. Business continuity: we'll share our DR drill reports under NDA
4. Insurance certificates: we'll share annually upon request
5. Background checks on our staff: we're solo — irrelevant

## Common customer redlines we don't accept

1. Unlimited liability — our cap is strict (12-month fees paid)
2. Mandatory source-code escrow — use the `.agent.md` spec instead
3. Custom indemnification beyond Section 13
4. Training-data use (explicitly blocked; Section 4.3)
5. Unlimited termination-for-convenience refunds beyond pro-rata
