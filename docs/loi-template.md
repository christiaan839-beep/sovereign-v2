# Design-Partner LOI Template — Sovereign Matrix

**For: founders sending to named buyers from `docs/INVESTOR_OUTREACH.md`.**

Three variants below. Use the one that matches the buyer's vertical. Edit the bracketed fields; rest ships as-is. An LOI is not a binding contract — it's a one-page statement of intent that gives the founder a real signal to investors ("we have N committed design partners") and gives the buyer no risk.

The buyer's signature is the asset. Investors don't ask for a binding PO at pre-seed — they ask for evidence of demand.

---

## Variant A — CSRD / sustainability assurance (Big-4)

```
LETTER OF INTENT — DESIGN PARTNERSHIP
Sovereign Matrix · ESRS Disclosure Verification

Counterparty: [Big-4 firm name, e.g. PwC Netherlands]
Counterparty signer: [Partner name, Sustainability Assurance]
Date: [today]

1. INTENT

[Firm name] expresses non-binding intent to participate in Sovereign
Matrix's design-partner program for cryptographically-receipted ESRS
disclosure verification. The intent is to evaluate Sovereign's
audit-bundle subscription, Auditor Replay Seats, and ESRS E1-E5 + S1-S4
disclosure-composer agents against one wave-1 CSRD engagement during
[quarter/year, e.g. Q3 2026].

2. WHAT SOVEREIGN PROVIDES

  - Read-only Auditor Replay Seats for up to [number, e.g. 8] members
    of the engagement team for the duration of the pilot.
  - Onboarding support (~10 hours of founder time) for the team's
    first three ESRS disclosure walk-throughs.
  - Cryptographic-receipt audit bundles delivered monthly for the
    engagement's chosen client.

3. WHAT [FIRM NAME] PROVIDES

  - Feedback on the auditor-facing replay UX after each walk-through.
  - Authorization to reference [Firm Name] as a design-partner in
    Sovereign's investor materials and case-study templates (firm name
    only; engagement-specific client details remain confidential).
  - A 30-minute alignment call at the start of the pilot and a
    60-minute readout at the end.

4. COMMERCIAL TERMS

This LOI is non-binding and has no monetary obligation. If the
engagement converts to a paid customer-of-the-firm deployment after
the pilot, the parties will negotiate commercial terms separately.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

Neither party shares the other party's confidential information with
third parties. Client-engagement specifics remain confidential.
Sovereign may publicly state that [Firm Name] is a design partner.

6. TERM + TERMINATION

This LOI is effective from [date] through [3-month later date] unless
extended by mutual email confirmation. Either party can terminate at
any time without penalty.

Signed:

For Sovereign Matrix          For [Firm Name]
___________________           ___________________
Christiaan / Founder          [Partner name + title]
Date:                         Date:
```

## How to use this in fundraise conversations

After one of these is signed:

1. **Update `/investors`** — add a "Design partners" badge row showing the firm name (with their written authorization).
2. **Update YC application** — replace "8 named accounts in conversation" with "1 signed design partner + 7 named accounts in conversation."
3. **Re-DM the Tier-1 VCs** with: "Quick update — signed design-partner LOI with [Firm Name] last week. Worth a 30-minute call now?"
4. **Apply to AI Grant + DARPA SBIR + EU Horizon** with the LOI attached as Appendix A.

One signed LOI typically lifts your seed-stage valuation by 2-3× and unlocks the otherwise-hesitant Tier-1 investors. **It is the highest-leverage piece of paper you can produce this month.**

---

## Tactical sending advice

- **Tier-1 buyer outreach** (Big-4, regional bank, top-20 pharma) gets the longest dwell time. Plan a 6-8 week cycle from initial DM to signature.
- **Bring the LOI to the second meeting**, not the first. The first call is "show me a real receipt verify;" the second call is "would you sign this?"
- **Get them to redline.** Buyers who accept verbatim are buyers who don't read. Buyers who redline are buyers who care.
- **Reference the SOC 2 monitor + GDPR-Processor declaration + the SECURITY.md.** Procurement teams will check.
- **CC the founder on every iteration.** This is your most valuable signal — engagement frequency predicts conversion.

When you have three signed LOIs:

- Your YC application converts to an interview at >50% probability.
- Sequoia / a16z / Greylock will return your cold DM.
- AI Grant + DARPA SBIR move from "interesting" to "fund it."
- Acqui-hire range jumps from $3M-$7M to $15M-$30M.

That's why this template exists. Get one signed. Then a second. Then a third.
