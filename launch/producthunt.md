## Description (Full)
We built an Autonomous Agency OS — not another chatbot wrapper.

**What makes this different:**

1. **132 specialized agents** that actually DO things — find leads, write SEO content, make voice calls at sub-200ms latency, analyze competitors, generate code, and deploy it. Not text generation. Real execution.

2. **51+ AI models with smart routing** — every task gets routed to the optimal model. DeepSeek for reasoning. Qwen for multilingual. Llama 4 Scout for massive documents. Claude Opus for deep analysis. If one model fails, the next picks up instantly.

3. **$0 per-token local execution** — run everything air-gapped on your own hardware via NVIDIA NemoClaw and Ollama. Your data never leaves your network. Zero API costs.

4. **Agent Teams that debate** — deploy 4 specialists that analyze a problem in parallel, critique each other's work (including a Devil's Advocate), and synthesize a unified plan.

5. **5-layer NeMo Guardrails** — jailbreak detection, PII scanning, hallucination detection, content moderation, and topic control. Enterprise-grade safety on every output.

6. **White-label ready** — agencies can rebrand the entire platform as their own. Custom domain, client portals, your logo. Business-in-a-box.
